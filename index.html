Ruisi Li

I am a PhD student in the RIDER Lab at Auburn University Dept of Computer Science and Software Engineering , working on Physiologically Grounded Models with Prof. Jiaqi Wang . My research focuses on multi-modal learning, representation learning, and foundation models for physiological dataset.

Prior to starting my PhD, I obtained an MS in Computer Engineering from New York University. I worked with Prof. Yao Wang and Prof. Adeen Flinker in the NYU Video Lab .

Beyond research, I really enjoy skiing ⛷️. Feel free to drop me an email if you would like to chat about LLMs, AI, or skiing 😊.
